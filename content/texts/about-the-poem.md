The *Stabat Mater Dolorosa* is one of the best-known medieval Latin hymns. It reflects on the suffering of Mary at the foot of the cross and inspired centuries of sacred music.

For this proof of concept, this page exists mainly to show that a standalone informational page type can be rendered cleanly in Hugo, with the same header, navigation, sidebar and footer structure as the rest of the site.

In a full migration, this section could contain the complete editorial material from the original website.
