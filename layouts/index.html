Search the Ultimate Stabat Mater Website

On the Ultimate Stabat Mater Website you will find the CD-collection of more than 300 different Stabat Mater compositions. Search for composers: alphabetically, chronologically, by country of origin and by duration. Listen to the compositions and watch videos of special performances. The site also includes translations of the original Latin poem in many languages.

Why this Stabat Mater website?

Initiator Hans van der Velden began this journey of musical discovery in 1992 with Haydn's Stabat Mater. He searched systematically for composers, performances on CD and translations of the original Latin poem. We are proud to continue his work.
