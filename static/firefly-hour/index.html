Firefly Hour

Click anywhere to release glowing fireflies into the garden. Hold and drag to paint trails. They flock, swirl around your cursor, and slowly turn the night into something alive.

Nice little trick: move the cursor slowly and they’ll orbit it. Flick fast and they scatter. It should feel a bit like a screensaver, a bit like a toy.
