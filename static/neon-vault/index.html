Neon Vault

You are a tiny thief inside a laser museum. Grab glowing shards, avoid sweeping drones, and dash through the exit before the whole vault wakes up.

Score0

Collect 8 shards, then reach the exit gate.

Move with arrow keys or WASD. Press Space for a short dash. On mobile, use the buttons. The lasers pulse; the drones drift; panic is part of the aesthetic.
